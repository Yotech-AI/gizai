# Mountains along the bottom of the window (GA-139)

Gizai's own wireframe landscape as a band along the bottom of the window, from the left edge to the right edge, under the
sidebar and the main column, for Jefsev to pick a strength. On Omarchy a little of the desktop's teal wireframe mountains
shows through the window; this draws mountains of our own, a bit more visible, never in the way of text or controls. A
frontend card builds the strength Jefsev picks; the website (GA-52) can use the hero.

- `bottom-band.html` is the design. Open it from disk in a browser: it uses the app's own stylesheets in `src/styles/`, so
  it shows the app's components, type styles and tokens. Every screen shows in dark, then light; the switches at the top
  show one theme or one strength.
- `gen-bottom-band.mjs` builds everything here from one seed and one list of colours: the two SVGs, `new-tokens.json`,
  `band-tokens.css`, the page and the tables below. It checks the colours first (the text's contrast over the band, the
  band apart from `live`, `needs` and the accent) and the SVGs' size (under 60 kB), and writes nothing when one fails.
  Run it with `node design/branding/gen-bottom-band.mjs`.
- `mountains.svg` is the app's band and `mountains-hero.svg` the website's hero: the same land from seed 139, the hero seen
  3.5 times closer up with a finer mesh. Their lines are `currentColor`, so one file serves both themes and every strength.
- `band.css` is the band's CSS (the layer, and which surfaces are see-through); the frontend card moves it into
  `src/styles`. `band-tokens.css` is the tokens as CSS for the page; `bottom-band-page.css` only lays out the page.
- `new-tokens.json` holds the band's tokens in `design/tokens.json`'s format.

Nothing in the app changes in this card.

## What the design decides

- **Our own land.** A height field, a flat valley between ridged mountains (gradient noise from a seeded generator), seen
  through a perspective camera and drawn as a mesh of four-sided cells. The lines a nearer slope hides are taken out, so the
  file holds only the lines you see. Far lines fade, and the top of the picture fades so the peaks melt into the page.
- **Sharp at every width, no seams.** One vector picture 4800 × 128 at 1:1, centred under the window's middle and cut at
  the sides (`preserveAspectRatio="xMidYMax slice"`), its lines 1px at every size (`vector-effect: non-scaling-stroke`).
  The valley is 300px wide at the bottom edge, so an 800px window still shows peaks on both sides.
- **The window's lowest layer.** One element, `.band`, the first child of `.app` at `z-index: -1` in the window's own
  stacking context. The canvases are see-through (the sidebar, the main column, the chat's threads and composer strip, the
  Editor's tree, the properties panel); the side-coloured ones keep their colour on a `::before` under the band. What holds
  content stays solid: cards, controls, the composer box, code and its status bar, menus and drawers. The band never runs
  behind code.
- **Not a status.** One cool blue, hue 228° in OKLCH: about 40° from teal (`live`, an agent at work) and from the accent's
  blue, with less chroma than either and a low alpha. It never moves. The terminal's rule holds (at least 35° and a ΔE2000
  of 15 from `live` and `needs`), and 30° from the accent.
- **Three strengths.** Subtle (about what shows through now), medium (the proposal) and strong (the bright strip of
  Jefsev's screenshot). Subtle and medium keep `text`, `text-2` and `text-3` at 4.5:1 between the lines at the band's
  bottom edge; strong drops `text-3` under 4.5:1 there (the table below). The focus ring keeps 3:1 in all three.
- **Height:** `clamp(var(--size-band-min), 14vh, var(--size-band))`, 80 to 128px: 126px in Gizai's first window (900 high),
  84px in its smallest (600). Under 560px high, with high contrast, in forced colours and on paper it goes.
- **Never in the way:** `pointer-events: none`, `user-select: none`, `aria-hidden="true"`; no motion, no space taken.
- **Settings → Appearance** gets a section, Background, with one checkbox, “Show mountains along the bottom”, on by
  default, kept on this computer as `gizai-band`, and on `<html>` as `data-band="on"`.
